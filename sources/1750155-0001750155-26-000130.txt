Charlotte’s Web Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Operating loss narrows to its lowest level since 2019 despite lower revenue, as costs and margins improve
Louisville, Colo., November 12, 2026 - (TSX: CWEB, OTCQX: CWBHF), Charlotte’s Web Holdings, Inc. (“Charlotte’s Web” or the “Company”), a botanical wellness innovation company and a market leader in cannabidiol (CBD) hemp extract wellness products, today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. All amounts are expressed in U.S. dollars unless otherwise indicated.
“Third-quarter revenue primarily reflected the restrictions on the sale of full-spectrum hemp products in California that took effect in July. We have since reintroduced broad-spectrum products in California and sales have steadily begun to recover there. Nationally, we have sharply reduced deep discounting so that our pricing reflects the premium quality and reliable efficacy customers expect from Charlotte’s Web. That lowered revenue in the near term, but it also improved margins and helped deliver our smallest operating loss since 2019,” said Bill Morachnick, Chief Executive Officer of Charlotte’s Web. “As we work to return to growth, we aim to build the business back on a more profitable footing through our refined customer acquisition and retention strategies. This includes focusing on channels including integrative healthcare and organizations participating in the CMS Innovation Center models.”
FEDERAL POLICY
Federal hemp policy continues to evolve legislative approaches to distinguish naturally occurring, non-intoxicating full-spectrum hemp products from synthetic and intoxicating cannabinoid products.
On September 2, 2026, following House passage, the Continuing Appropriations and Extensions Act, 2027 (Public Law 119-103) was signed into law, delaying enactment of federal hemp restrictions contained in Section 781 of Public Law 119-37 as they apply to non-synthetic hemp products to December 11, 2026. Unless amended, on December 11, 2026 the law will restrict hemp products containing more than 0.4 milligrams of total THC per container. Under current law, the Company’s full-spectrum products would fall within these restrictions while its broad-spectrum and isolate products are expected to remain generally available. There are a number of members of Congress who wish to adjust Section 781 to protect full-spectrum hemp products, either through the process of passing government funding or by one of many bipartisan bills that have been introduced, including the HEMP Act and the Lawful Hemp Protection Act. Charlotte’s Web continues to advocate, directly and through the ONE HEMP coalition, for a framework that distinguishes non-intoxicating full-spectrum products from synthetic and intoxicating products and preserves access for the consumers who have relied on full-spectrum products for years. The Company supports rigorous testing, accurate labeling, traceability, enforcement against bad actors and safeguards for minors, and favors science-based regulation over prohibition. Despite its advocacy, the Company cannot predict whether or when Congress will act.
HEALTHCARE CHANNEL
The national healthcare program pilot under the Substance Access Beneficiary Engagement Incentive (“BEI”) allows participating Accountable Care Organizations (“ACOs”) to provide eligible hemp-derived products to Medicare beneficiaries, funding up to $500 per beneficiary per year themselves rather than through Medicare reimbursement. The Company is in negotiations with ACOs regarding a potential pilot expected to begin in 2027. CMS's current product definition accommodates the Company's full-spectrum products, and CMS has stated it will adjust its requirements to follow federal law. No revenue has been recognized from the BEI to date, and the Company does not anticipate meaningful BEI-related revenue in 2026.
To make it easier for practitioners to recommend and supply its products, Charlotte's Web has launched a dedicated ordering portal for healthcare practitioners and a new affiliate program. Practitioners can now have products shipped directly to their patients or stock them in their clinics, giving patients immediate access.
The Company has spent several months laying the groundwork to participate in the Medicare Advantage channel, developing healthcare relationships, evaluating benefit pathways, and preparing the infrastructure needed to support potential program implementation, which to date has been restricted by the lack of regulatory clarity. As the regulatory framework for hemp-derived products gains clarity, the Company believes this preparation could position it to pursue opportunities with Medicare Advantage plans and other healthcare partners seeking compliant pathways to provide eligible hemp-derived products to their members.
COMMERCIAL AND OPERATIONS
Charlotte’s Web has reintroduced broad-spectrum products in California, where revenue has begun to recover since the July restrictions, regaining approximately 40% of the sales lost following the restrictions, and has simplified its product range, as reflected in the Q3 inventory charges.
The Company’s Route CBD activation traveled 1,750 miles along the California-Nevada border, meeting and supporting families who had lost normal access to full-spectrum products under the California restrictions. The initiative drew strong consumer response and supportive trade coverage.
In August, the Company announced a partnership with online marketplace and retailer Edibles.com, which now carries a selection of Charlotte’s Web products online and at its flagship store in Atlanta. The companies are also working together to advocate for federal standards that preserve access to non-intoxicating hemp products.
Since the end of the third quarter, as December 11 approaches, consumer concern has grown, with increased customer care calls, advocacy letters and inquiries about the Company’s Original Formula products as more families plan ahead. Consumers have sent more than 42,000 letters to Congress asking it to preserve access to full-spectrum hemp products.The Company expects some customers to purchase ahead of December 11, which may shift sales between periods. Some competitors have exited the market, and others may follow, which could affect the Company’s share of a smaller category. The net effect on future sales cannot yet be determined, and the Company does not view near-term purchasing as indicative of ongoing demand.
DEFLORIA
DeFloria, Inc., in which Charlotte’s Web owns approximately one-third, is developing AJA001 Oral Solution, an investigational full-spectrum botanical drug candidate. Charlotte’s Web holds exclusive commercial manufacturing rights for AJA001 upon potential FDA approval. Charlotte’s Web carries its investment in DeFloria at fair value, of $8.4 million at September 30, 2026. The Phase 2 program in irritability associated with autism spectrum disorder remains substantially advanced, and its initiation remains subject to securing the resources required to fund it. DeFloria is also evaluating rare pediatric neurological indications for AJA001, each of which would be expected to be eligible for orphan drug designation. A peer-reviewed paper reporting the safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetic results of DeFloria's completed Phase 1 trial of AJA001 has been accepted for publication in The Journal of Clinical Pharmacology.
THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL REVIEW
“This was our first full quarter without the convertible debenture, and the balance sheet is in a very different place than it was at the start of the year,” said Erika Lind, Chief Financial Officer. “Total liabilities are $19.4 million, down from $77.3 million at December 31, 2025, and shareholders’ equity is $53.1 million, compared with a deficit of $2.0 million at year-end. We prioritized margin over volume this year. Gross margin was 47.0% for the first nine months, despite a significant inventory reserve to adjust the product portfolio. Third-quarter SG&A was $8.1 million, 17% lower than a year ago. Part of the SG&A reduction reflects spending we have held back until the federal outcome is clear. With higher margins, more of our revenue recovery should reach the bottom line.”

Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $9.4 million, down 18.3% from $11.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, and 13.5% from $10.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. The decrease primarily reflects the California hemp restrictions that took effect in July 2026 and the Company’s reduced online
promotional discounting, a simplified product range and a general softening in demand across the hemp wellness category in certain states. Revenue in California has begun to recover since the Company reintroduced broad-spectrum products there.
Gross profit was $4.4 million, or 46.9% of revenue, compared with $4.5 million, or 38.9% of revenue, in the third quarter of 2025 and 47.5% in the second quarter of 2026. Year-over-year gross margin improved due to the Company’s revised promotional strategy, improved pricing and mix, lower standard costs from insourcing, and the absence of insourcing start-up costs incurred in the third quarter of 2025. Cost of goods sold also included a $1.4 million addition to the inventory reserve related to simplifying the Company’s product line, largely offset by one-time inventory valuation adjustments. The Company continues to target a gross margin of approximately 50%, although quarterly results may vary with inventory adjustments and the outcome of federal rules.
SG&A was $8.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $9.7 million in the third quarter of 2025, a 16.8% decrease reflecting lower more targeted paid media spending and reduced initial debt amortization costs following the debenture conversion in May. SG&A was 14.9% lower than in the second quarter of 2026 as third-quarter spending was held back pending clarity on federal rules.
Operating loss was $3.7 million, compared with $5.3 million in the third quarter of 2025 and $4.4 million in the second quarter of 2026. Net loss was $3.5 million, or $(0.01) per share, compared with $5.8 million, or $(0.04) per share. The third quarter included a non-cash gain of $0.1 million from the change in fair value of the Company’s investment in DeFloria, compared with a non-cash charge of $1.2 million in the prior year that related primarily to the convertible debenture. Per-share amounts reflect the higher share count following the debenture conversion and private placement in May.
Adjusted EBITDA1 was a loss of $0.7 million, compared with a loss of $2.2 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Nine-Month Financial Review
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenue was $31.4 million, down 14.1% from $36.6 million in the prior-year period. Gross margin improved to 47.0% from 45.7%, and SG&A decreased 13.5% to $27.1 million from $31.4 million. Operating loss improved 15.7% to $12.4 million from $14.7 million. Net loss was $20.7 million, or $(0.10) per share, compared with $18.3 million, or $(0.12) per share, and included a $3.2 million net non-cash fair value charge, mostly related to the debenture before its conversion in May, and a $4.2 million non-cash loss on extinguishment of debt on that conversion.
Financial Position
Cash and cash equivalents were $11.4 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $14.0 million at June 30, 2026, and working capital was $23.8 million, compared with $21.7 million at December 31, 2025. The decrease in cash partly reflects a portion of the Company’s annual business insurance premium, which is paid in the third quarter of each year. Net cash used in operating activities was $2.6 million in the third quarter and $6.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $12.2 million in the prior-year period.

About Charlotte’s Web Holdings, Inc.
Charlotte's Web Holdings, Inc., a Certified B Corporation headquartered in Louisville, Colorado, is a botanical wellness innovation company and a market leader in hemp extract wellness that includes Charlotte's Web whole-plant full-spectrum CBD (cannabidiol) extracts as well as broad-spectrum CBD and other naturally occurring cannabinoid isolates, terpenes, flavonoids, and other beneficial compounds. Charlotte's Web product categories include CBD oil tinctures (liquid products), CBD gummies (sleep, calming, exercise recovery, immunity), CBN gummies, functional mushroom gummies, CBD capsules, CBD topical creams, and lotions. Through its substantially vertically integrated business model, Charlotte's Web maintains stringent control over product quality and consistency with analytical testing from soil to shelf for quality assurance. Charlotte's Web products are distributed to retailers and healthcare practitioners throughout the U.S.A. and are available online through the Company's website at www.charlottesweb.com.

These non-GAAP financial measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, our reported financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Specifically, we use these non-GAAP measures as measures of operating performance; to prepare our annual operating budget; to allocate resources to enhance the financial performance of our business; to evaluate the effectiveness of our business strategies; to provide consistency and comparability with past financial performance; to facilitate a comparison of our results with those of other companies, many of which use similar non-GAAP financial measures to supplement their GAAP results; and in communications with our board of directors concerning our financial performance.
