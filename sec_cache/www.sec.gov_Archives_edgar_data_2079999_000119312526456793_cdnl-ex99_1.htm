Cardinal Infrastructure Group Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Company reports strong revenue growth and outlines actions to strengthen execution and profitability

Raleigh, NC – November 12, 2026 – Cardinal Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CDNL) ("Cardinal" or the "Company"), today announced the Company’s third quarter and year-to-date 2026 financial results.

"Revenue grew 65.1% this quarter to $201.9 million, but our third-quarter Adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 4.2%, well below our expectations," said Jeremy Spivey, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. "The decline primarily reflected project estimate revisions, residential pricing pressure, lower initial bid margins on certain commercial and industrial work, and higher fuel and transportation costs. These results are not acceptable, and they make clear that we need to strengthen how we estimate, manage and execute projects across Cardinal."

"As a second-generation operator with more than 30 years of experience in this industry, I know what strong project execution requires, and I recognize when we need to raise our standard. We are taking several actions to do just that. Benji Wood, our Chief Operating Officer, is a third-generation operator with more than 30 years of experience and a demonstrated record of success leading ALGC. Benji will now take a more direct role in establishing and implementing consistent operating standards across the Cardinal platform, focusing on project selection, estimating, project reviews and accountability. We will also engage an independent third-party advisor to support implementation of these operational changes and assess our estimating and project review processes."

"Separately, in collaboration with Hughes Brothers, we've decided to postpone the proposed acquisition. We believe the right priority today is strengthening execution across our existing operations and realizing the benefits of the businesses we've already acquired. We also remain focused on capital allocation. Given the strength of the balance sheet, our Board of Directors has authorized a share repurchase program of up to $50 million of our Class A common stock over the next 12 months. Our strategy remains intact. Our immediate priority is strengthening execution and building the operating discipline and supporting infrastructure necessary to translate growth into more consistent profitability."

Third Quarter Results

Revenues increased $79.6 million, or 65.1%, to $201.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $122.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Revenue growth included $23.9 million of organic growth, primarily reflecting higher residential project volumes and continued expansion across the Company's geographic footprint, with the remaining $55.7 million increase attributable to acquired businesses.

Cost of revenues increased $88.2 million, or 85.8%, to $191.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $102.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to organic and acquisition-related growth and higher fuel and transportation costs.

Gross margin decreased 10.5 percentage points to 5.4% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from 15.9% for the three months ended September 30, 2025. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, as additional project information became available, the Company revised its estimates of costs to complete on certain projects, resulting in project estimate revisions that reduced gross profit by $13.2 million. Gross margin was also negatively impacted by residential pricing pressure, lower initial bid margins on certain commercial and industrial projects, and higher fuel and transportation costs.

General and administrative expenses increased $9.6 million, or 187.0%, to $14.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $5.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to $3.2 million non-cash stock-based compensation, $2.3 million of expenses from acquired businesses, $1.8 million of acquisition-related costs, and investments in personnel, systems and infrastructure to support the growth and increased complexity of the organization.

The Company reported a net loss of $11.0 million, compared to net income of $10.1 million in the prior year period. Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter was $8.5 million, reflecting an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 4.2%, compared to $21.4 million and 17.5%, respectively, in the prior-year period. The 13.3-percentage-point decline in Adjusted EBITDA margin primarily reflected project estimate revisions, which accounted for approximately 6.5 percentage points of the decline; residential pricing pressure and lower initial bid margins on certain commercial and industrial work, which accounted for approximately 3.6 points; and higher fuel and transportation costs, which accounted for approximately 3.5 points. These impacts were partially offset by approximately 0.3 points from other factors.

 

 

 

 

Year-To-Date Results

Revenues increased $286.1 million, or 92.2%, to $596.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from $310.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Revenue growth included $144.1 million of organic growth, primarily reflecting higher residential project volumes and continued expansion across the Company's geographic footprint, with the remaining $142.0 million increase attributable to acquired businesses.

Cost of revenues increased $264.9 million, or 100.9%, to $527.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from $262.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, reflecting organic and acquisition-related growth and higher fuel and transportation costs.

Gross margin decreased 3.9 percentage points to 11.5% for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from 15.4% for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily reflecting higher fuel and transportation costs, the project estimate revisions recorded during the third quarter, residential pricing pressure, and lower initial bid margins on certain commercial and industrial projects.

General and administrative expenses increased $23.7 million to $34.1 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 from $10.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to $5.3 million of expenses from acquired businesses, $4.9 million of acquisition-related costs, $3.7 million of non-cash stock-based compensation, and investments in personnel, systems, and infrastructure to support the growth and increased complexity of the organization.

Net income was $11.7 million, compared to $26.2 million in the same period of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $63.4 million, reflecting an Adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.6%, compared to $55.7 million and 17.9%, respectively, in the prior-year period.

Select Balance Sheet and Liquidity Information

As of September 30, 2026, Cardinal had $335.0 million in cash and cash equivalents and $198.5 million in total debt, representing $136.5 million of net cash. The Company also had $100.0 million of availability under its revolving credit facility and $250.0 million available under the delayed draw term loan. Net cash provided by operating activities increased to $30.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $26.3 million in the prior-year period. Capital expenditures were $41.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $31.7 million in the prior-year period.

Share Repurchase Program

The Company’s Board of Directors has authorized a share repurchase program under which the Company may repurchase up to $50 million of its Class A common stock over the next 12 months. Repurchases, if any, may be made from time to time based on market conditions, the Company’s capital requirements and other factors. The program does not obligate the Company to repurchase any particular amount of Class A common stock, and the program may be suspended, modified or discontinued at any time.

Backlog

Cardinal's total backlog as of September 30, 2026, was $815.5 million, a 26.2% increase from the prior-year period, primarily reflecting the ALGC acquisition.

Guidance

The Company is withdrawing its previously issued full-year 2026 revenue and Adjusted EBITDA margin guidance. While the Company currently expects fourth-quarter Adjusted EBITDA to improve from the third quarter, it is not providing a specific revenue or margin outlook.

Leadership Transition

On November 9, 2026, Mike Rowe notified the Company of his decision to resign from his position as Chief Financial Officer upon the appointment of his successor. Mr. Rowe will continue to serve in his current position until his successor joins the Company. The Company has engaged Spencer Stuart to conduct a comprehensive search for his successor.

Conference Call

Cardinal management will discuss results and outlook during its quarterly investor conference call today starting at 10:30 a.m. ET. The call and accompanying slide presentation will be webcast on the "Events & Presentations" section of Cardinal’s website. A replay of the webcast will be available at the same location shortly after the conclusion of the presentation.

About Cardinal

Cardinal Infrastructure Group Inc. (NASDAQ: CDNL) is one of the Southeast's fastest-growing, full-service infrastructure service providers. The Company delivers integrated civil and site development solutions across high-growth markets through a self-performing model supported by skilled labor, specialized fleets and market-leading subsidiaries. This model enables efficient, turnkey project

 

execution at scale while maintaining focus on building long-term client relationships. Cardinal's strategy is grounded in operational discipline, market expansion and a commitment to integrity from the ground up.

Non-GAAP Measures

Cardinal presents results of operations in a way that it believes will be the most meaningful and useful to investors, analysts, rating agencies and others who use Company financial information to evaluate performance. Certain of these financial measures are not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("U.S. GAAP") and are considered non-GAAP under SEC rules and regulations. For example, in this press release, Cardinal presents organic revenue growth, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Free Cash Flow, Net (cash) debt, Net (cash) debt / Adjusted EBITDA and Interest Coverage, all of which are non-GAAP financial measures as defined in our Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025, our most recent Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, and in our other filings with the SEC. These non-GAAP financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only and are not intended to be substitutes for any GAAP financial measures, including net income, and, as calculated, may not be comparable to companies in other industries or within the same industry with similarly titled measures of performance. In addition, these non-GAAP measures should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items. Therefore, non-GAAP financial measures should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, or in isolation from, measures prepared in accordance with GAAP.

Reconciliation to Non-GAAP Measures

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization ("EBITDA"), adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization ("Adjusted EBITDA"), Free Cash Flow, and Net (cash) debt are not measures of results of operations, cash flows from operations or indebtedness under U.S. GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to other U.S. GAAP measurements. We have presented organic revenue growth, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Free Cash Flow, and Net (cash) debt to adjust for the items identified below because we believe that it would be helpful to the readers of our financial information to understand the impact of these items on our reported amounts. This presentation enables readers to better compare our results to similar companies that may not incur the impact of various items identified above. Management acknowledges that there are many items that impact a company's reported results or operating cash flows and these lists are not intended to present all items that may have impacted these items. Organic revenue growth, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Free Cash Flow, Net (cash) debt, and any ratios calculated based on these measures are not necessarily comparable to similarly-titled measures used by other companies or appearing in our debt obligations or agreements. Organic revenue growth, EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow as presented may not equal column or row totals due to rounding.

We define EBITDA as net income for the period adjusted for interest expense, net income tax expense, depreciation and amortization expense. Adjusted EBITDA further adjusts EBITDA for certain expenses associated with non-routine transactions, including (i) transaction fees and acquisition-related costs incurred in connection with acquisitions and planned acquisitions, (ii) non-routine costs associated with legal matters in which the Company is a defendant, (iii) certain consulting and recruiting costs related to acquisitions and public company readiness, (iv) non-routine revenue impact from customer claims, (v) non-routine loss on extinguishment and refinancing costs, (vi) stock-based compensation, (vii) non-routine IPO related travel and compensation, (viii) remeasurement losses recorded in other expense net, and (ix) other non-routine gains and charges that we do not believe reflect our underlying business performance. We define Adjusted EBITDA margin as Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenue. We define Net (cash) debt as total debt less cash and cash equivalents and Net (cash) debt / Adjusted EBITDA as Net (cash) debt as a multiple of Adjusted EBITDA. We define Interest Coverage as Adjusted EBITDA as a multiple of interest expense, net. We define Free Cash Flow as net cash provided by (used) in operating activities less Capital Expenditures, net of disposals.
