Arcutis Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update
•Continued strong ZORYVE® (roflumilast) net product revenue growth, with Q3 revenues of $143.9 million, +45% versus Q3 of 2025, and +11% versus Q2 of 2026, driven by strong demand growth
•Raised 2026 full-year net product sales guidance to $535 million–$545 million
•Partnered with World Champion gymnast Simone Biles to launch the Skin to Believe In awareness campaign for individuals living with inflammatory skin conditions
•Remains on track to disclose topline results from Phase 2 trial of ZORYVE foam 0.3% in individuals with vitiligo and decision on program advancement in Q4 of 2026
•Maintained positive operating cash flow for the quarter with $18.9 million of net cash provided by operating activities
Westlake Village, CA, November 4, 2026 – Arcutis Biotherapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ARQT), a commercial-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing meaningful innovations in immuno-dermatology, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“Yet another strong quarter of performance reflects the consistent execution of our corporate strategy and further strengthens our financial position. ZORYVE continues to lead the branded non-steroidal topical segment driven by its efficacy and tolerability across multiple inflammatory skin conditions and patient populations. We are very excited to launch a new awareness campaign with Simone Biles, a World Champion gymnast who uses ZORYVE herself, to raise awareness of inflammatory skin diseases and of advanced targeted topicals like ZORYVE,” said Frank Watanabe, president and chief executive officer. “Looking ahead, with a potential FDA approval of ZORYVE cream 0.05% for infants as young as 3 months in the first quarter of 2027, the progress of our indication expansion trials, and the advancement of our ARQ-234 Phase 1 trial, we are well positioned to drive continued growth.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Updates
Commercial Highlights
ZORYVE — a highly potent and selective phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) inhibitor in once-daily cream and foam formulations, approved in the United States and Canada for the treatment of plaque psoriasis, atopic dermatitis, and seborrheic dermatitis.
•ZORYVE net product sales for the third quarter of 2026 were $143.9 million, reflecting 45% year-over-year growth and an 11% sequential increase versus the second quarter of 2026. The year-over-year and sequential increases were driven by increasing demand across products.
•Launched the Skin to Believe In campaign with Simone Biles to raise awareness of seborrheic dermatitis and eczema and to encourage individuals to talk to their doctor about long-term, steroid-free, topical treatment options for their skin conditions, including ZORYVE.
Clinical and Regulatory Developments
•The Company initiated a pivotal Phase 3 trial with ZORYVE cream 0.3% for the treatment of adults and children with moderate to severe atopic dermatitis, distinct from the current indication of ZORYVE cream 0.15% for the treatment of adults and children 6 years of age and older with mild to moderate atopic dermatitis.
•The FDA has set a target action date of February 23, 2027, for the supplemental New Drug Application to expand the indication for ZORYVE cream 0.05% for mild to moderate atopic dermatitis to infants aged 3 to 24 months.
•The Company continues to enroll patients in a Phase 4 study evaluating quality and speed of itch improvement with ZORYVE cream 0.15% in adult patients with atopic dermatitis (INTEGUMENT-ITCH).
•The Company remains on track to disclose topline results from the Phase 2 proof-of-concept study of ZORYVE foam 0.3% for the treatment of vitiligo and share its decision on program advancement by the end of 2026.
•The Company continues to enroll patients in the Phase 2 proof-of-concept studies of ZORYVE foam 0.3% for the treatment of hidradenitis suppurativa, and expects to report results, as well as a decision on program advancement, in the first quarter of 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Summary Financial Results
Product revenues for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $143.9 million compared to $99.2 million for the corresponding period in 2025, an increase of 45%. All products grew in the quarter, with revenues for the quarter of $37.8 million for ZORYVE cream 0.3%, $27.3 million for ZORYVE cream 0.15%, $3.7 million for ZORYVE cream 0.05%, and $75.1 million for ZORYVE topical foam 0.3%. The year-over-year and quarter-over-quarter increases were predominantly due to increased unit demand, as well as modest improvements in gross-to-net (GTN) deductions.

Cost of sales for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $12.5 million compared to $8.7 million for the corresponding period in 2025, due to increasing ZORYVE sales volume.

Research and development (R&D) expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $22.4 million compared to $19.6 million for the corresponding period in 2025. The year-over-year increase was primarily driven by ARQ-234 clinical development expense and investment in medical affairs to support medical education related to our roflumilast programs.

Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 were $99.6 million compared to $62.4 million for the corresponding period in 2025. The year-over-year increase was driven by greater marketing spend primarily in support of the Skin to Believe In celebrity awareness campaign and by increased personnel-related expenses resulting from the dermatology sales force expansion and launch of the primary care provider and pediatric sales team.

Net income was $8.8 million, or $0.07 basic and $0.06 diluted earnings per share, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to net income of $7.4 million, or $0.06 basic and diluted earnings per share, for the corresponding period in 2025.

Cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and marketable securities were $259.8 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $221.3 million as of December 31, 2025. Net cash provided by operating activities was $18.9 million during the third quarter.

Financial Guidance
•The Company raised net product sales guidance for the full year 2026 to $535 million–$545 million from $525 million–$540 million.

About Arcutis
Arcutis Biotherapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ARQT) is a commercial-stage medical dermatology company that champions meaningful innovation to address the urgent needs of individuals living with immune-mediated dermatological diseases and conditions. With a commitment to solving the most persistent patient challenges in dermatology, Arcutis has a growing portfolio of advanced targeted topicals approved to treat three major inflammatory skin diseases. Arcutis’ unique dermatology development platform coupled with our dermatology expertise allows us to develop differentiated therapies against biologically validated targets, and has produced a robust pipeline for a range of inflammatory dermatological conditions.
