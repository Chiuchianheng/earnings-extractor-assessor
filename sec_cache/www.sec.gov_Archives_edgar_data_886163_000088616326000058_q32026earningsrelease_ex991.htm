Ligand Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Third-quarter royalty revenue up 51% year-over-year to $70.3 million

Raises Full-Year 2026 Adjusted Diluted EPS Guidance to $9.50 to $9.75, from $9.00 to $9.501; Reaffirms Revenue Guidance

Conference call begins at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time today

JUPITER, Fla., October 29, 2026 – Ligand Pharmaceuticals Incorporated (Nasdaq: LGND) today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided an operating forecast and business update. Ligand management will host a conference call and webcast today at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss the results and answer questions.
“Ligand delivered another strong quarter, with royalty revenue growing 51% year-over-year, driven by contribution from the XOMA portfolio and continued commercial momentum across our key royalty assets, including Filspari,” said Todd Davis, CEO of Ligand. “We also continued to put capital to work in differentiated assets, acquiring a royalty interest in Santen’s Ryjunea and entering into a structured financing agreement with AvenCell Therapeutics to advance its next-generation CAR-T pipeline. With a strong balance sheet and a portfolio of over 200 assets, we are well positioned to continue executing our royalty aggregation strategy, and we look forward to sharing an updated five-year outlook at our investor day on December 8th.”
Financial Results
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Third-quarter 2026 results reflect continued strength in the royalty business, with royalty revenue increasing 51% year-over-year to $70.3 million, compared with $46.6 million for the prior-year period. Royalties accounted for 89% of total revenues and income in the quarter, with growth driven by the newly acquired XOMA assets and continued momentum across Ligand’s key commercial portfolio, including Travere Therapeutics’ Filspari and Merck’s Ohtuvayre.
Total revenues and income for the third quarter of 2026 were $78.7 million, compared with $115.5 million for the prior-year period, representing a decrease of 32%. The prior-year period included $53.1 million of Pelthos divestiture-related income (see Pelthos note below). Excluding that income, total revenues and income increased 26%. Captisol® sales were $7.9 million, compared with $10.7 million, reflecting the timing of customer orders. Contract revenue and income were $0.5 million, compared with $58.2 million, with the decrease primarily due to the prior-year Pelthos divestiture-related income.
Cost of Captisol was $2.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $3.8 million for the prior-year period, with the decrease primarily attributable to lower Captisol sales. Amortization of intangibles was $10.2 million, compared with $8.1 million for the prior-year period, with the increase attributable to the intangibles acquired from the XOMA acquisition. Research and development expense was $2.8 million, compared with $21.0 million for the prior-year period, with the decrease primarily attributable to a $17.8 million charge recognized in the prior-year period related to the research and development funding arrangement associated with the AVIM Therapy

Year-to-Date Financial Results
Royalty revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased 46% to $161.3 million, compared with $110.5 million for the prior-year period, and accounted for 83% of total revenues and income. Royalty growth was primarily driven by the newly acquired XOMA assets and continued momentum across Ligand’s key commercial portfolio, including Travere Therapeutics’ Filspari and Merck’s Ohtuvayre. Total revenues and income were $194.2 million, compared with $208.4 million, representing a decrease of 7%. The prior-year period included $53.1 million of Pelthos divestiture-related income (see Pelthos note above). Excluding that income, total revenues and income increased 25%. Captisol sales were $24.6 million, compared with $32.4 million, reflecting the timing of customer orders. Contract revenue and income were $8.3 million, compared with $65.5 million, with the decrease primarily due to the prior-year Pelthos divestiture-related income.
Cost of Captisol for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $9.1 million, compared with $11.6 million for the prior-year period, with the decrease primarily attributable to lower Captisol sales. Amortization of intangibles was $26.4 million, compared with $24.6 million for the prior-year period, with the increase attributable to the intangibles acquired from the XOMA acquisition. Research and development expense was $19.6 million, compared with $77.7 million for the prior-year period, reflecting several prior-year charges: $44.3 million for the research and development funding arrangement associated with the D-Fi royalty rights acquired in the Castle Creek transaction, $17.8 million for the arrangement associated with the AVIM Therapy royalty rights acquired in the Orchestra BioMed transaction, and $6.4 million related to Ligand’s former Pelthos business. This was partially offset by $12.3 million of research and development funding arrangement expense recognized in the current-year period related to Orchestra BioMed. General and administrative expense was $122.6 million, compared with $67.4 million for the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflects transaction and integration costs and share-based compensation associated with the XOMA acquisition, as well as higher employee-related costs, including increased headcount, from the expansion of Ligand’s portfolio management and origination platform to scale its larger royalty portfolio.
Net non-operating expense was $14.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with net non-operating income of $76.5 million for the prior-year period. The change was primarily attributable to non-cash mark-to-market losses on equity-method investments and other investments during the nine months ended


September 30, 2026, compared with non-cash gains recognized in the prior-year period, partially offset by higher gains on short-term investments and higher net interest income.
GAAP net income was $6.7 million, or $0.31 per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $79.7 million, or $3.95 per diluted share, for the prior-year period. Results for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 included a $49.2 million pre-tax, non-cash loss from change in fair value of equity-method investments and other investments.
Adjusted net income was $142.9 million, or $6.67 per diluted share, compared with $122.4 million, or $6.07 per diluted share, for the prior-year period, representing year-over-year increases of 17% and 10%, respectively. The increase was primarily attributable to the 46% increase in royalty revenue, higher net interest income and realized gains on short-term investments, partially offset by higher general and administrative expense. Adjusted Financial Measures
Ligand reports adjusted net income and adjusted earnings per diluted share in addition to, and not as a substitute for, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP, and does not consider such measures superior to GAAP results. The Company also reports “core” versions of these measures, which exclude any gains on the sale of the Pelthos business.
Adjusted earnings per diluted share is a key component of the financial metrics utilized by the Company’s board of directors to evaluate management performance and determine certain elements of management compensation. GAAP results include items such as share‑based compensation expense, amortization of acquisition‑related and intangible assets, changes in contingent liabilities, mark‑to‑market adjustments on investments in public companies, transaction‑related costs and related tax effects, which are excluded from adjusted results and are detailed in the reconciliations included at the end of this press release.
This press release includes adjusted total revenues and income, which excludes all $53.1 million of Pelthos-related income recognized in the prior-year period, including the portion attributed to out-license fees. Ligand believes the adjusted total revenues and income is a more meaningful indicator of its business performance than the GAAP total revenues and income.
Liquidity and Capital Resources
As of September 30, 2026, Ligand had cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments of $447.4 million, compared with $733.5 million at December 31, 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by cash used for the XOMA acquisition, partially offset by the proceeds from the Company’s issuance of its convertible senior notes due 2031. As of September 30, 2026, including its equity investments and availability under its credit facility, Ligand has approximately $750 million of deployable capital available to pursue additional royalty acquisitions and strategic investments.

Third Quarter Corporate Highlights
On July 14, 2026, Ligand completed the acquisition of XOMA Royalty, a biotechnology royalty aggregator. The acquisition strengthens Ligand’s royalty portfolio by adding seven commercial products, including Roche’s VABYSMO® (faricimab-svoa), Servier and Ipsen’s OJEMDA™ (tovorafenib), and Zevra Therapeutics’ MIPLYFFA® (arimoclomol). Additionally, the acquisition adds 14 late-stage development programs, featuring Takeda’s mezagitamab and certain assets from Takeda’s externalized asset portfolio, including osavampator, volixibat, and OHB-607, along with more than 100 additional assets in various stages of development to Ligand’s portfolio. As a result, Ligand’s portfolio has more than doubled in size, now comprising over 200 commercial, clinical, and preclinical stage royalty assets.
On September 22, 2026, Ligand announced the acquisition of a milestone and royalty interest in Santen Pharmaceutical’s Ryjunea from Sydnexis for a $23 million upfront payment. Under the terms of the agreement, Ligand acquired a 100% interest in certain payments and related rights under Sydnexis’s existing license agreement with Santen. The acquired payment rights include a tiered low-double-digit to high-teens royalty on Ryjunea net sales in Europe, the Middle East and Africa, as well as certain milestone payments.
On September 24, 2026, Ligand announced it entered into a financing agreement with AvenCell Therapeutics, Inc., a clinical-stage cell therapy company developing controllable, allogeneic CAR-T therapies for patients with cancer, for up to $47 million. The investment is expected to help advance AvenCell’s pipeline, including AVC-201 for the treatment of relapsed/refractory acute myeloid leukemia (AML) and AVC-203 for the treatment of B-cell malignancies. Under the terms of the agreement, Ligand has committed up to $41 million in exchange for a mid single-digit to low double-digit royalty on worldwide annual net sales of all current and future AvenCell pipeline assets, with the applicable rate determined based on the total amount ultimately funded. The capital will be funded in four tranches, with the first tranche payable at closing, and the remaining three tranches payable upon achievement of certain predetermined clinical milestones and other specified financing conditions. Ligand has also committed up to $6 million in AvenCell’s concomitant Series C financing.

Key Portfolio Developments
Qtorin rapamycin
•On August 4, 2026, Palvella announced that it plans to initiate a Phase 3 trial of Qtorin rapamycin for the treatment of cutaneous venous malformations in the fourth quarter of 2026. Palvella is planning a resubmission to the FDA for Breakthrough Therapy Designation incorporating TOIVA patient interview transcripts and new 24-week data.
•On October 28, 2026, Palvella announced the FDA accepted for filing and granted Priority Review of the New Drug Application (“NDA”) for Qtorin rapamycin for the treatment of microcystic lymphatic malformations (“microcystic LMs”). The FDA has assigned a Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target action date of February 28, 2027. Palvella is preparing for a potential U.S. commercial launch in the first half of 2027, subject to FDA approval.
AVIM Therapy/VIRTUE SAB
•On August 10, 2026, Orchestra BioMed highlighted several recent business updates:
◦The FDA granted AVIM Therapy a second FDA Breakthrough Device Designation (“BDD”) specific to patients with uncontrolled hypertension despite anti-hypertensive medication who are indicated for a pacemaker.
◦The Virtue Sirolimus AngioInfusion (“Virtue SAB”) pivotal trial is advancing with further site activations and patient enrollments.
•On October 1, 2026, Orchestra BioMed announced it surpassed full enrollment in the BACKBEAT global pivotal trial of AVIM Therapy with 330 patients randomized, exceeding its previously announced target of 316 randomized patients.
Zelsuvmi
•On August 13, 2026, Pelthos announced several recent business updates:
◦11,925 Zelsuvmi units were dispensed by 4,571 unique prescribers during the second quarter of 2026, representing a 48% quarter-over-quarter increase.
◦More than 25,000 patients have now been prescribed Zelsuvmi since it was commercially launched in July 2025.
◦Pelthos expects to launch Xepi in the first quarter of 2027.
Lasofoxifene
•On August 14, 2026, LeonaBio announced it is on track to complete enrollment of approximately 600 patients in the Phase 3 Elaine-3 clinical trial evaluating lasofoxifene in ER-positive (ER+), HER2-negative, ESR1-mutated metastatic breast cancer in the fourth quarter of 2026 with topline data expected in the second half of 2027.
Ojemda
•On September 16, 2026, Ipsen announced the Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (“MHLW”) approved Ojemda for low-grade glioma harboring a BRAF mutation or fusion gene.
Volixibat

•On August 5, 2026, Mirum Pharmaceuticals announced volixibat was granted Breakthrough Therapy Designation for cholestatic pruritus due to primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC). Additionally, a pre-NDA meeting was held for volixibat in cholestatic pruritus due to PSC and additional discussions are planned before potential NDA submission.
•On August 5, 2026, Mirum Pharmaceuticals announced enrollment was completed in the VANTAGE Phase 2b study of volixibat in cholestatic pruritus due to primary biliary cholangitis (PBC) with topline results expected in the first quarter of 2027.


Ersodetug
•On September 9, 2026, Rezolute provided an update on the FDA’s review of data from its Phase 3 sunRIZE study of ersodetug for hypoglycemia due to congenital hyperinsulinism (“HI”). The study’s open-label extension is ongoing, with high participation and several indicators of improved glycemic control, including a notable reduction in the use of background standard-of-care therapies.
•On October 5, 2026, Rezolute announced that following a preliminary review of detailed data from the Phase 3 sunRIZE study of ersodetug for the treatment of hypoglycemia due to congenital HI, the FDA recommended a pre-BLA meeting with Rezolute as a precursor to submitting a BLA.
•On October 14, 2026, Rezolute announced completion of enrollment in the Phase 3 study of ersodetug in tumor HI and topline data is expected mid-December 2026.
Soticlestat
•On August 13, 2026, Ovid announced a newly formed portfolio company with funds advised by Perceptive Advisors acquired global rights to soticlestat. Ovid secured equity in the company and the potential to receive regulatory and sales milestone payments and royalties upon success. Ligand is entitled to royalties and a share of milestones received by Ovid.
IV ganaxolone
•On October 26, 2026, Immedica announced that it will advance the development of intravenous (IV) ganaxolone for the treatment of refractory status epilepticus (RSE) and plans to initiate a new phase 3 clinical study in 2027. Ligand is entitled to royalties, milestones and revenue from Captisol material sales.

About Ligand Pharmaceuticals
Ligand is a leading royalty aggregator, partnering with biopharmaceutical companies to finance and advance late-stage clinical development programs. Ligand owns and manages one of the largest and most diversified portfolios of biopharmaceutical royalties in the industry, with economic interests in more than 200 development and commercial-stage assets. Ligand funds high-value programs in exchange for long-term economic interests, aligning capital with clinical and commercial success. Ligand’s royalty portfolio is designed to deliver consistent and predictable revenue streams across a broad range of therapeutic assets. Ligand also licenses its proprietary technologies, Captisol® and NITRICIL™, to support drug development and formulation across its global partner network. For more information, visit www.ligand.com or follow Ligand on X and LinkedIn.
