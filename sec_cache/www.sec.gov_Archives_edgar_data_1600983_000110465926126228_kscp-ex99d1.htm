Knightscope Reports Third Quarter Revenue Up 177% Year Over Year to $8.7 Million

Third consecutive quarter of triple-digit year-over-year revenue growth

Gross margin improved $2.0 million year over year to a positive $0.4 million

Sunnyvale, Calif. — November 12, 2026 — Knightscope, Inc. (NASDAQ: KSCP) (the "Company" or "Knightscope"), the security technology company building the nation's first Autonomous Security Force, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Revenue was $8.7 million, up 177% year over year, marking the Company's third consecutive quarter of triple-digit year-over-year revenue growth.

"We delivered our third consecutive quarter of triple-digit revenue growth, and positive gross margin in our services business," said William Santana Li, Founder, Chairman and CEO of Knightscope. "This year we redefined our go-to-market strategy around delivering security outcomes - autonomous machines, AI-driven software, and licensed security agents operating as one accountable operation under one contract. Our priorities are clear: convert the approximately $27 million in renewals, bookings, and client awards secured since May 2026 into revenue, expand with the clients we already serve, and drive margins higher.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

•Revenue: $8.7 million, up 177% from $3.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Service revenue was $8.6 million, up 349%, including $6.6 million from Knightscope Security Force ("KSF"). Product revenue was $0.1 million, compared with $1.2 million in the prior-year period.
•Gross margin: $0.4 million, or 5% of revenue, compared with a gross loss of $1.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Service gross margin was $0.7 million, or 8% of service revenue, compared with a service gross loss of $1.6 million, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Operating expenses: $12.0 million, or 138% of revenue, compared with $7.9 million, or 254% of revenue, in the prior-year period. Research and development expense declined 5% to $3.6 million as the Company continued moving engineering in-house to improve development speed and control, while reducing development costs. Sales, general and administrative expense was $8.4 million, including $0.9 million of events and sponsorship costs, primarily for the Autonomous Security Force unveiling at GSX 2026, and $0.6 million of higher intangible asset amortization related to the KSF acquisition.
•Net loss: $11.8 million, or $0.50 per share, based on 23.6 million weighted average shares, compared with $9.5 million, or $0.98 per share, based on 9.8 million weighted average shares, in the third quarter of 2025.
•Balance Sheet: Cash and cash equivalents of $6.8 million at quarter end.

​

Business and Operational Highlights

•Unveiled the Autonomous Security Force at GSX 2026, presenting autonomous machines, AI-driven software, and licensed security agents as one managed service under one contract.
•Secured approximately $27 million in renewals, bookings, and client awards since May 2026, a Company record.
•K7 manufacturing commenced at Knightscope Headquarters ("KHQ") to accelerate deployment during the fourth quarter 2026.
•Signals, the Company’s orchestration software and operational digital twin, is alive in its initial release at KHQ and demonstrated live to the public last week.
