Kyverna Therapeutics Announces Completion of BLA Submission for Miv-cel in Stiff Person Syndrome and Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

BLA submission for miv-cel in SPS completed with request for priority review

 

Commercial readiness advancing for potential U.S. launch of miv-cel, which would become the first approved therapy for SPS and first approved CAR-T therapy in autoimmune disease

 

KYSA-6 Phase 3 gMG trial enrollment on-track for completion in mid-2027

 

Miv-cel’s clinical profile continues to set the benchmark for autoimmune CAR T, demonstrating durable clinical benefit and a consistent, well-tolerated safety profile across multiple neurologic autoimmune indications

 

EMERYVILLE, Calif., November 12, 2026 -- Kyverna Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: KYTX), a late-stage clinical immunology company pioneering transformative therapies for people with neurologic autoimmune diseases, today reported portfolio progress and financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Kyverna is very proud to submit the first BLA for a CAR T-cell therapy in autoimmune disease, paving the way for a potential new standard of care with a single dose, designed to reset the immune system and free patients from lifelong disease and chronic immunotherapies,” said Warner Biddle, Chief Executive Officer of Kyverna. “We believe miv-cel’s clinical profile, differentiated construct design and established manufacturing process can support a strong launch in SPS, if approved. We continue to advance our commercial readiness activities by hiring key commercial leaders with deep expertise in CAR T, neurology, and rare disease launches, initiating the critical field infrastructure, and working with treatment centers to prepare for potential patient access. Our progress to date is a testament to the dedication of our employees, investigators, patients, and partners. SPS is our lead indication, but we believe it is only the beginning of a much broader opportunity to transform the treatment paradigm for neurologic autoimmune diseases.”

 

Execution of Miv-cel BLA Submission for SPS Seeking Priority Review

•
Kyverna has completed the submission of its rolling Biologics License Application (BLA) to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), seeking priority review of miv-cel (mivocabtagene autoleucel, KYV-101) in stiff person syndrome (SPS) under its Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation. The BLA submission is based on a comprehensive positive data package from the KYSA-8 registrational trial in patients with SPS.
•
In anticipation of a potential FDA approval and commercial launch in 2027, Kyverna is rapidly progressing launch readiness activities through targeted site activation engagements with CAR-T experts, neurologists and payers, and building an integrated patient support and reimbursement network to streamline the treatment journey. With experienced leaders in place, recruiting efforts continue for a focused group of field-based sales and medical specialists.

 

 

•
One-year SPS data reported at MS Toronto in October 2026 demonstrated miv-cel reversed disability and achieved durable efficacy across all primary and secondary endpoints, with nearly all patients remaining off chronic immunotherapies with a single dose. In addition, miv-cel continues to demonstrate a consistent and well-tolerated safety profile, with no high-grade cytokine release syndrome (CRS) or immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS), and no cases of immune effector cell-associated hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis-like syndrome (IEC-HS). The primary analysis from KYSA-8 were recently published in the Journal of American Medical Association (JAMA) Neurology, and additional one-year biomarker data from KYSA-8 will be presented at ASH 2026.

 

Progress in Miv-cel Neuroimmunology Franchise

•
Enrollment On Track in KYSA-6 Registrational Trial for Generalized Myasthenia Gravis (gMG):
o
Phase 2 longer-term follow up data presented in September 2026 and at the American Association of Neuromuscular & Electrodiagnostic Medicine (AANEM) Annual Meeting highlight that miv-cel provided a robust and durable effect for up to 1.5 years, with the majority of patients maintaining minimal symptom expression (MSE) and staying free of chronic immunotherapies after a single dose. In addition, miv-cel demonstrated a consistent and well-tolerated safety profile with no high-grade CRS, no ICANS, and no reported cases of IEC-HS.
o
The robust longer-term follow up data continue to de-risk the Phase 3 gMG trial, with enrollment expected to be completed in mid-2027.

 

•
Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (PMS):
o
Updated Phase 1 data from an Investigator-initiated trial (IIT) conducted by Stanford University, which were presented at MS Toronto further supported that miv-cel improved disability scores while achieving robust CAR T penetration in the central nervous system (CNS) without the need for chronic immunotherapies. Durable results were achieved beyond two years in the first patient treated, underscoring the durability of treatment effect achieved with a single dose of miv-cel. Miv-cel continues to be well-tolerated with no high-grade CRS and no reported cases of ICANS or IEC-HS.
o
RMAT designation was granted by the FDA for miv-cel in non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis (naSPMS). Kyverna expects to share its development strategy in PMS by early 2027.

 

•
KYV-102 Program Update:
o
Kyverna has elected to deprioritize its KYV-102 program as the Company continues to focus on accelerating its late-stage neurologic indications with miv-cel. The Company will continue to advance lifecycle management activities for miv-cel to broaden access for patients and lower costs.

 

 

 

Financial Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

•
Kyverna reported $170.6 million in cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities as of September 30, 2026, which, in addition to amounts available for draw from the Oxford Finance amended loan facility, is expected to provide operating runway into 2028.
•
Research and Development (R&D) expenses were $27.0 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•
General and Administrative (G&A) expenses were $15.9 million for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•
For the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported a net loss of $41.9 million, or a net loss per common share of $0.68.

 

About Miv-cel (mivocabtagene autoleucel, KYV-101)
Miv-cel is a fully human, autologous, CD19-targeting CAR T-cell therapy with CD28 co-stimulation. It is uniquely designed for potency and tolerability with the potential to achieve deep B-cell depletion, reset the immune system, and deliver durable drug-free, disease-free remission in autoimmune diseases with a single dose. Miv-cel is under investigation for B-cell driven autoimmune diseases and is produced using a well-established, validated manufacturing process. To date, more than 100 patients have been treated with miv-cel across a range of autoimmune diseases, and the clinical data demonstrates a consistent and well-tolerated safety profile.

 

About Kyverna Therapeutics
Kyverna Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: KYTX) is a late-stage clinical immunology company pioneering differentiated therapies with curative potential for neurologic autoimmune diseases. Kyverna’s lead autologous CD19-targeting CAR T-cell therapy candidate, miv-cel (mivocabtagene autoleucel, KYV-101), has demonstrated the potential to fundamentally change the treatment paradigm across multiple B-cell-driven autoimmune diseases. Kyverna is advancing its potentially first-in-class neuroimmunology franchise with its recently submitted Biologics License Application (BLA) for miv-cel in stiff person syndrome (SPS) and an ongoing registrational trial for generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG).

 

Miv-cel has received three FDA Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designations, in SPS, gMG, and non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis (naSPMS) based on compelling clinical data and significant unmet need, further reinforcing the therapy's potential across neuroimmunology. Additionally, the Company continues to advance new innovations that broaden access and choice for patients, expanding its leadership position in the field. For more information, please visit https://kyvernatx.com.
