PURE Bioscience Reports Fiscal 2026

Financial Results

 

El CAJON, CA – PURE Bioscience, Inc. (OTCQB: PURE) (“PURE,” the “Company,” or “we”), creator of the patented non-toxic silver dihydrogen citrate (SDC) antimicrobial, today reported financial results for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2026.

 

Summary of Results – Year-End Operations

 

●Net product sales were $2,315,000 and $2,198,000 for the fiscal years ended July 31, 2026 and 2025, respectively. The $117,000 increase was attributable to increased sales across our end-user network.
●Net loss for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2026 was ($2.5 million), compared to ($2.4 million) for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2025. During the prior fiscal year, net loss was offset by $175,000 received from the U.S. Government’s Employee Retention Tax Credit Program.
●Net loss, excluding share-based compensation, was ($2.3 million) for the fiscal years ended July 31, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
●Net loss per share was ($0.02) for the fiscal years ended July 31, 2026 and 2025, respectively.
●Net cash used in operations for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2026 was ($1.97 million), compared to ($2.0 million) for the fiscal year ended July 31, 2025.

 

Jeff Kitchell, Chief Executive Officer, stated, “We are up year-over-year despite being a challenging year, with the macro environment causing increased expenses and foodborne outbreaks severely disrupting the food industry. Although our growth didn’t meet expectations, our focus remains on reducing costs and converting more opportunities into revenue. We continue to strengthen and expand our technology across our partnerships and to provide our distributor network with technical support and marketing tools for the industries they serve. Our product penetration in the dairy industry has not met expectations due to slow customer adoption and industry consolidation. We are strengthening all our business development models, and adjustments are underway that better serve our customers and partners. We continue to prove that our SDC products are superior to the current chemistries in use.”

 

About PURE Bioscience, Inc.

 

PURE is committed to redefining chemical safety through its innovative technology. With a focus on efficacy and effectiveness, PURE develops advanced solutions that meet the highest safety standards and produce best-in-class results for its consumers and distributors. PURE continues to focus on developing and commercializing our proprietary antimicrobial products, primarily in the food and beverage industry. We provide solutions to combat the health and environmental challenges posed by pathogens and ensure hygienic control. Our technology platform is based on patented, stabilized ionic silver, and our products contain silver dihydrogen citrate, better known as SDC. This broad-spectrum, non-toxic antimicrobial agent formulates well with other compounds. As a platform technology, SDC is distinguished from existing products in the marketplace because of its superior efficacy, reduced toxicity, and mitigation of bacterial resistance. Additional information on PURE is available at www.purebio.com.
